# Sky Hop

Free single-file HTML5 one-tap flying game. Flap a glowing bird through pipe
gaps that shrink as your score climbs — the sky melts from day to dusk to
night as you go. Bronze, silver and gold medals at 10/25/50 points. One HTML
file, zero assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/sky-hop/

- Tap / click / Space to flap, procedural WebAudio sounds
- Day-to-night sky gradient, parallax clouds and hills, feather-burst effects
- Procedural visuals, no dependencies, no tracking
