# Sky Hop — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Tap to flap a glowing bird through pipe gaps that shrink as you score. Chase
bronze, silver and gold medals while the sky melts from day to dusk to night.
One tap to play, forever to master.

(191 characters)

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / One-tap action
- Controls: Tap / click / Space to flap. That's the whole game.
- Features: Medal system (10/25/50 = bronze/silver/gold), best-score saving,
  day→dusk→night sky gradient, parallax clouds and hills, screen shake and
  feather-burst effects, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file HTML5
  (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
